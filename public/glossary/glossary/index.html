    Glossary

    An A-Z of horological terms used across Escapement: amplitude to vph, with links to the pages that explain each one properly.

    The vocabulary of horology is French-inflected and unapologetic. This glossary covers the terms this site uses, with links to the full treatment.

    Amplitude — degrees the balance swings per beat; the watch’s vital sign. → Amplitude

    Balance wheel — weighted wheel on a staff; with the hairspring, the oscillator. → The Balance Assembly

    Barrel — drum holding the mainspring; often the train’s first wheel. → The Mainspring and Barrel

    Beat — one half-oscillation; “vibrations per hour” counts beats. → Frequency

    BPH / vph — beats (vibrations) per hour: 18,000 / 21,600 / 28,800 / 36,000. → Frequency

    Calibre — a movement architecture/designation. → How to Read a Calibre

    Cannon pinion — friction-fit pinion driving the motion works. → The Motion Works and Dial

    Chronograph — stopwatch mechanism integrated in a watch. → The Chronograph

    Column wheel — turret controlling chronograph levers. → Column wheel vs cam

    Complication — any function beyond hours/minutes/seconds. → What Counts as a Complication

    COSC — Swiss chronometer certification (−4/+6 s/day). → Positions and Rate

    Detent escapement — chronometer escapement; direct impulse, high accuracy. → The Detent Escapement

    Drop — free fall of an escape tooth between pallets. → Drop, Lock, and Lift

    Endshake / sideshake — axial/radial play of a wheel in its settings.

    Epilame — surface treatment holding oil in place. → Friction and Lubrication

    Escape wheel — the train’s last wheel; drives the escapement. → The Swiss Lever Escapement

    Fusee — cone pulley equalizing mainspring torque. → Constant-force tricks

    Gear train — barrel to escape wheel; ratio and power transport. → The Gear Train

    Hairspring / balance spring — the oscillator’s spring. → The Balance Spring

    Isochronism — constant period across amplitudes. → Isochronism

    Jewels — synthetic ruby bearings; friction and wear control. → Friction and Lubrication

    Keyless works — winding/setting mechanism at the crown. → The Keyless Works

    Lift angle — geometry used to compute amplitude; movement-specific. → Lift

    Lock — engagement depth of an escape tooth on a pallet. → Drop, Lock, and Lift

    Lever escapement — the standard detached wristwatch escapement. → The Swiss Lever Escapement

    Mainspring — the watch’s spring battery. → The Mainspring and Barrel

    Minute repeater — strikes hours, quarters, minutes on demand. → The Minute Repeater

    Moonphase — lunar phase display, 59-tooth or better. → The Moonphase

    Perpetual calendar (QP) — calendar with leap-year memory. → The Perpetual Calendar

    Power reserve — stored run time; also the gauge showing it. → Power Reserve

    Rate — seconds per day, fast (+) or slow (−). → Positions and Rate

    Remontoir — small constant-force spring re-wound at intervals. → Constant-force tricks

    Shock setting — spring-mounted balance jewel (Incabloc etc.). → Shock settings

    Tourbillon — rotating cage averaging positional error. → The Tourbillon

    vph — vibrations per hour (see BPH). → Frequency

    Going barrel — barrel that is also the first train wheel. → The Mainspring and Barrel
